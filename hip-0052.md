# `hip-0052` - Mark spell levels in the Spell Book

> **Category:** Spell Book UI

In the **Spell Book**, group spells under clearly labeled `Level 1` through `Level 5` separators and show a compact `L1`–`L5` marker on every spell entry. Keep the marker visible on every page and under every spell-school or spell-type filter, even when the relevant separator is outside the visible page.

This must make a spell's level recognizable before the player attempts to cast it. For example, mark `Hypnotize` as `L3`. Because `Azure Dragons` are immune to spells of levels `1`–`3`, the marker immediately shows that `Hypnotize` cannot affect them. This distinguishes spell-level immunity from the separate failure case in which another target stack contains too many creatures for `Hypnotize`'s total maximum-health limit, even though both cases currently produce the same generic rejection message. Killing enough creatures can make the latter stack eligible; it can never overcome the `Azure Dragon` spell-level immunity.

Label this explicitly as the `spell level`; do not confuse it with `Basic`, `Advanced`, or `Expert` proficiency in a school of magic. Do not change spell ordering, availability, cost, targeting, or mechanics; this proposal changes only the **Spell Book** presentation.
