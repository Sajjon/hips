# `hip-0083` - Show the ranged damage penalty boundary

> **Category:** Combat UI

While holding `Shift` and hovering over a `shooter` in combat, show a clear boundary separating its normal-range hexes from hexes subject to the distance damage penalty. Add this to the existing movement preview without obscuring it.

Use the game's [range-penalty calculation](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Range_penalty), normally the ten-hex boundary, and distinguish distance from obstacle or melee penalties. For the player's own shooter, account for known effects that remove the penalty and state when no distance penalty applies.

For an enemy shooter, show the standard boundary or normally disclosed exceptions without exposing hidden artifact effects. Label that boundary as standard when the enemy's actual modifiers are unknown. The overlay changes information presentation only.
