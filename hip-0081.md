# `hip-0081` - Preview retaliation damage before melee attacks

> **Category:** Combat UI

When hovering over a legal melee attack, show the defender's expected `retaliation` damage range beside the attacker's damage range. Calculate it from the defenders that could survive the proposed attack, their remaining retaliations, the attack direction, and relevant visible abilities and effects.

Show **No retaliation** when the attack prevents retaliation, the defender cannot retaliate, or every possible ordinary attack result destroys the defender. Where attack damage varies, show a range covering the resulting survivor counts; label any estimate whose special-effect outcomes cannot be represented exactly.

Follow normal [retaliation rules](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/No_enemy_retaliation). Do not reveal a future random roll or hidden enemy equipment, and do not treat reflected damage such as `Fire Shield` as retaliation damage.
