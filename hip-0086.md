# `hip-0086` - Sell resources down to a chosen reserve

> **Category:** Town UI

In the **Marketplace**, add a **Keep** quantity field for the source resource. Entering `20` for `crystal`, for example, must prepare the largest legal sale that leaves at least `20` crystal in the player's stockpile.

Calculate the trade using the current [Marketplace exchange rate](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Marketplace) and its indivisible trade batches. Show the amount sold, proceeds, and actual remaining stock before confirmation; if an exact reserve is impossible, retain the smallest reachable amount above it. If current stock is already at or below the reserve, prepare no sale.

Require the usual trade confirmation. This complements [`hip-0021`](hip-0021.md), which specifies a desired quantity of the resource being purchased.
