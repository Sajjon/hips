# `hip-0048` - Mark towns with garrison heroes

> **Category:** Adventure Map UI

Use the `Town Portal` destination selector's existing presentation of towns containing `garrison heroes` as the precedent, and expose the same presence information in the main **town list** on the **Adventure Map**.

Unlike the `Town Portal` destination selector, do not show the `garrison hero`'s portrait, name, or identity in the main **town list**. Instead, show only a small, discreet, persistent indicator that the town has a `garrison hero`. The indicator must be visible on both selected and unselected town entries without obscuring the town portrait or other existing information.

Show the indicator only for a hero occupying the town's `garrison hero` position. A `visiting hero` or creatures in the town's `garrison` must not trigger it. Update the indicator immediately when a hero enters or leaves the garrison, the visiting and garrison heroes swap positions, or the town changes ownership.
