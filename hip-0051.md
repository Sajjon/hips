# `hip-0051` - Navigate Kingdom Overview towns with arrow keys

> **Category:** Kingdom Overview UI

While **Kingdom Overview** is displaying **Town Information**, pressing `Up Arrow` must move the town list to the previous town and pressing `Down Arrow` must move it to the next town. Keep **Kingdom Overview** open and do not open the corresponding **Town Screen**.

Use the current town-list order and make these shortcuts behave exactly like the existing **Scroll Up** and **Scroll Down** controls, including their behavior at the beginning and end of the list. When not every town fits on screen, scroll the list by one town so the newly reached entry becomes visible. Apply these shortcuts only to **Town Information**; do not change arrow-key behavior while **Hero Information** is displayed.
