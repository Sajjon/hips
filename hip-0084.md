# `hip-0084` - Show remaining retaliations in creature information

> **Category:** Combat UI

In a combat stack's right-click information window, show its remaining `retaliations` for the current round. Display a numerical count for finite retaliation and **Unlimited** when the stack can retaliate without a round limit.

Use the current [retaliation state](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/No_enemy_retaliation), including spent retaliations and visible effects such as `Counterstrike`. Refresh after attacks, dispels, and round changes. Distinguish a stack that has exhausted its allowance from one whose retaliation is temporarily prevented by another visible effect.

For enemy stacks, preserve existing information boundaries and do not identify hidden equipment as the source of a modifier.
