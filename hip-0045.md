# `hip-0045` - Target spells and ranged attacks from the battle queue

> **Category:** Combat UI

While the player is choosing a target for a single-stack `spell` or a ranged attack by a `shooter`, make every valid enemy `creature stack` in the **battle queue** an alternate target. Hovering over a queue entry must highlight its stack on the battlefield and show the same targeting cursor and validity feedback as hovering over that stack directly.

Clicking a valid queue entry must cast a stack-targeted `spell` or perform a ranged attack exactly as clicking the corresponding battlefield stack would. Apply the same targeting restrictions, immunities, attack mode, range and obstacle penalties, confirmation behavior, and action cost regardless of where the target is clicked. If the queue contains more than one entry for the same stack, every such entry must refer to that one battlefield target.

Do not enable queue targeting for melee attacks, because the player must choose an attack hex and direction on the battlefield. Spells and abilities that target a hex, area, obstacle, or other non-stack entity must also continue to be targeted on the battlefield.
