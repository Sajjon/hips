# `hip-0045` - Target enemy stacks from the battle queue

> **Category:** Combat UI

While the player is choosing a target for a single-stack `spell` or attack, make every valid enemy `creature stack` in the **battle queue** an alternate target. Hovering over a queue entry must highlight its stack on the battlefield and show the same targeting cursor and validity feedback as hovering over that stack directly.

Clicking a valid queue entry must cast a stack-targeted `spell` or perform a ranged attack exactly as clicking the corresponding battlefield stack would. Apply the same targeting restrictions, immunities, attack mode, range and obstacle penalties, confirmation behavior, and action cost regardless of where the target is clicked. If the queue contains more than one entry for the same stack, every such entry must refer to that one battlefield target.

For a melee attack, clicking the queue entry must select the enemy stack and show its legal attack hexes and directions on the battlefield; the player must then choose one before the attack is executed. Never choose a movement path, attack hex, or direction automatically. Spells and abilities that target a hex, area, obstacle, or other non-stack entity must continue to be targeted on the battlefield.
