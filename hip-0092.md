# `hip-0092` - Rearrange troops in the Hill Fort

> **Category:** Creature recruitment UI

Allow the player to reorder and merge their army stacks directly in the **Hill Fort** interface. Use the normal army-slot interactions for moving a stack to an empty slot and combining stacks of the same creature type, without requiring the player to close and reopen the upgrade screen.

Refresh the displayed upgrade options, quantities, and prices after each change. Preserve creature counts and normal army restrictions. Moving or merging stacks must not automatically upgrade them or spend resources.

Apply the same interface behavior to the [standard Hill Fort](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Hill_Fort) and *Horn of the Abyss* variants while retaining each object's actual upgrade rules.
