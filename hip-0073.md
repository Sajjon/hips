# `hip-0073` - Show hero-specific spell effects on hover

> **Category:** Spell Book UI

In the **Spell Book**, hovering over a spell must show its calculated effect for the displayed hero, including the hero's current `Spell Power`, magic-school proficiency, specialty, and applicable known bonuses. Show numerical damage, summoned creature count, duration, or another variable quantity where relevant.

For [Slow](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Slow), show the current proficiency's speed-reduction rule and targeting scope; a target's final speed can be shown once that target is known. For [Summon Air Elemental](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Summon_Air_Elemental), show the number of creatures the hero would summon.

Distinguish values known before targeting from values that depend on a particular target, resistance, or hidden enemy information. Refresh the preview when the displayed hero or their effective spell modifiers change.
