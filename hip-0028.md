# `hip-0028` - Show enemy morale without revealing hidden sources

> **Category:** Combat UI

The HD Mod already shows a detailed `morale` breakdown for the player's own `creature stacks`. Extend the combat UI to show useful `morale` details for enemy stacks without revealing the enemy hero's hidden information.

At the start of combat, show each enemy stack's exact pre-combat `morale` baseline and effective starting value. Combine every enemy-private source into a single **Other pre-combat modifiers** entry. Do not reveal or categorize whether that value comes from `Leadership`, equipped `artifacts`, visited adventure map objects, army composition, or any other hidden enemy state.

Itemize only modifiers whose sources are already known to the player:

- Every `morale` modifier caused by the player's own hero, including equipped `Ring of Suppression`, `Hideous Mask`, or `Pendant of Downfall` artifacts.
- Every visible spell or effect applied after combat begins, including `Mirth` and `Sorrow`; show its value and remaining duration where applicable.

Update the enemy stack's current effective `morale` whenever one of these known modifiers changes. Preserve the game's normal caps, immunity, and negation behavior.

For example, an enemy stack's panel could show **Other pre-combat modifiers: +2**, **Pendant of Downfall (your hero): -2**, and **Starting effective morale: 0**. If `Sorrow` then reduces its `morale` by `-2`, show `Sorrow` separately and update **Current effective morale** to `-2` without exposing the sources behind the original `+2`.
