# `hip-0034` - Show spell level and school

> **Category:** Combat Log UI

For every `spell` cast during combat, show its `school of magic` before its casting proficiency—`Basic`, `Advanced`, or `Expert`. For the player's own cast, use a label such as `[Earth Magic • Expert level]`. If an opposing hero's proficiency is not already known to the player, show **Unknown Level**, as in `[Water · Unknown Level]`, rather than exposing hidden hero information.

Render the `spell` name in *italics* and color it by its `school of magic`, matching the labels in the **Spell Book**: `Fire Magic` red, `Earth Magic` green, `Water Magic` blue, and `Air Magic` purple.

Enclose each styled spell name in single quotation marks, such as `'Slow'` or `'Clone'`, including when the name appears in a spell-effect entry.

See the [combined combat-log proof of concept](hip-0030.md#proof-of-concept) for the proposed spell styling, proficiency labels, and school colors alongside the other log improvements.
