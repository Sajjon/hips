# `hip-0067` - Mark weekly recruitment at map dwellings

> **Category:** Creature recruitment UI

Show a persistent **Recruited this week** indicator in the hover or right-click information for discovered `external dwellings` and `Refugee Camps` after any of the current player's heroes successfully recruits creatures there. Distinguish this from merely visiting the object or opening and canceling its recruitment dialog.

Reset the indicator at the start of each new week. A partial recruitment still marks the visit as a recruitment; where remaining stock is normally known, continue showing it separately so the marker does not imply that the object is empty. Save and restore the player's recruitment history with the game.

Follow the normal [dwelling growth](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Growth) and [Refugee Camp](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/List_of_adventure_map_objects) rules. The marker reports the player's own actions and must not disclose recruitment by an unseen opponent.
