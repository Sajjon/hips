# `hip-0036` - Identify damage sources and all targets

> **Category:** Combat Log UI

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Depends on:** [`hip-0029`](hip-0029.md)

For every damaging attack, spell, ability, or battlefield effect recorded in the **combat log**, identify its source and every affected `creature stack` using the labels from [`hip-0029`](hip-0029.md). The source may be an attacking stack, casting hero, creature caster, `war machine`, spell, or battlefield peril. Report the damage and number of creatures that perish separately for each stack; do not collapse an area-of-effect action into a combined total. Include secondary, adjacent, and friendly-fire targets, as well as affected stacks that take zero damage because of an immunity, and preserve the order in which the game resolves them.

For example: “Black Dragons (`F`) attack Black Dragons (`b`), inflicting 7,286 damage; 5 creatures perish. Dragon breath also hits Titans (`c`), inflicting 1,200 damage; 4 creatures perish.” Apply the same structure whenever one action affects multiple stacks.
