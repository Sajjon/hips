# `hip-0096` - Preview the total exchange value of resources

> **Category:** Town UI

In the **Marketplace**, show the total amount of the selected destination resource the player could have after exchanging all other current resources directly into it at the current rates. Include the existing stock of the destination resource in the total.

For each source resource, use the normal [Marketplace trade batches and rates](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Marketplace), rounding down to the number of complete legal batches. Show any unsold remainders. Refresh the preview when resources, destination, or applicable rates change.

This is a valuation preview for planning purchases or construction. It must not sell resources automatically or assume that favorable multi-step exchange routes were used.
