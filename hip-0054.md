# `hip-0054` - Automate new-day artifact loadouts

> **Category:** Hero UI — artifacts

Add an automatic artifact-management pass around the new-day boundary for every hero controlled by a human player. It must reproduce beneficial manual artifact juggling without leaving the hero's normal equipment changed.

Immediately before resolving a player's start-of-day effects, save each hero's exact equipped artifacts and `combination artifact` assembly state in a private, temporary per-hero `costume`. This snapshot must not read, overwrite, or otherwise affect the *HD Mod* `Costumes` slots `0`–`9`. Build a temporary loadout using only artifacts equipped by or carried in that hero's `backpack`; never transfer artifacts between heroes.

Choose each temporary loadout using the following priorities. A higher priority must never be sacrificed for a lower one:

1. **Priority 0a — land movement slots:** When the hero will start the day on land, maximize effective start-of-day `movement points` in the `feet slot` and two `ring slots` before considering `Knowledge` or income in those slots. Equip `Boots of Speed` and one effective `Equestrian's Gloves` whenever available. Apply normal stacking rules; equipping a duplicate that grants no additional movement is not beneficial.
2. **Priority 0b — full spell points at maximum reachable Knowledge:** Consider this priority only when the hero's current `spell points` are below the greatest maximum the hero can reach with a valid temporary loadout after satisfying priority 0a, and that loadout can fully replenish the hero. Full replenishment is available when the hero will start the day in a town with a `Mage Guild`, or when the loadout can equip an assembled `Wizard's Well`. Maximize `Knowledge` in every remaining compatible slot, calculate maximum `spell points` using the hero's actual `Intelligence` level and specialty, and equip `Wizard's Well` when the town will not provide the replenishment. If current `spell points` already equal or exceed that greatest reachable maximum, skip this priority and preserve the excess.
3. **Priority 1 — water movement:** When the hero will start the day in a boat, use slots not reserved by priority 0b to maximize water `movement points`. Equip `Admiral's Hat`, or whichever of `Sea Captain's Hat` and `Necklace of Ocean Guidance` can legally fit, without displacing a higher-priority artifact.
4. **Priority 2a — gold and precious-resource income:** Fill remaining compatible slots with artifacts that generate `gold`, `mercury`, `sulfur`, `crystal`, or `gems` each day. Use the actual daily output, including combination bonuses and the normal stacking of resource-producing artifacts. Within the same resource, prefer the greater output. When two loadouts trade different priority-2a resources and neither produces less of every resource, prefer the one requiring fewer changes from the hero's saved equipment rather than inventing an exchange rate.
5. **Priority 2b — wood and ore income:** Only after priority 2a is maximized, fill any remaining slots with artifacts that generate `wood` or `ore`. Never reduce `gold` or precious-resource income merely to gain `wood` or `ore`.

For priority 0b, define the decision using the engine's real spell-point calculation rather than a hard-coded multiplier:

```text
refillLoadouts = validLoadouts
  .respecting(priority0a)
  .where(startsInTownWithMageGuild || equips(WizardsWell))

targetMaxSpellPoints = max(refillLoadouts.map(maxSpellPointsIncludingIntelligence))
needsFullReplenishment = currentSpellPoints < targetMaxSpellPoints
```

For example, a hero without `Intelligence` who currently has `10 Knowledge` and `100 spell points` is not full for this purpose if a valid loadout can raise `Knowledge` to `13` and fully replenish spell points. Equip that Knowledge loadout and `Wizard's Well`, if needed, so the hero starts the day with `130 spell points`. Do not replace `Boots of Speed` or `Equestrian's Gloves` to gain that extra Knowledge.

Evaluate both assembled and disassembled forms of every relevant `combination artifact`. Automatically assemble `Wizard's Well`, `Golden Goose`, `Cornucopia`, or another combination when its combined effect improves the highest applicable priority. Automatically disassemble one when its components produce a better legal result. For example, if `Cornucopia` cannot be equipped because one component slot is reserved for a Knowledge-boosting artifact under priority 0b, or because a `ring slot` is reserved for `Equestrian's Gloves`, keep the higher-priority artifact, disassemble `Cornucopia`, and equip as many non-conflicting resource-producing components as possible. Likewise, if a town's `Mage Guild` will fully replenish spell points, do not occupy slots with `Wizard's Well` when those slots can instead generate income.

After all start-of-day movement, spell-point, and artifact-income effects have been resolved, immediately restore every hero's exact saved equipment and original assembled or disassembled state. The temporary pass must be atomic and lossless, must work with a full `backpack`, and must not visibly cycle through heroes or equipment. The calculated `movement points`, restored `spell points`, and generated resources remain in effect for the new day.

Conceptually:

```text
for hero in currentHumanPlayer.heroes:
    snapshot[hero] = saveTemporaryCostume(hero)
    equip(hero, optimizeNewDayLoadout(hero))

resolveStartOfDayEffects()

for hero in currentHumanPlayer.heroes:
    restoreExactEquipment(hero, snapshot[hero])
```
