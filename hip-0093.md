# `hip-0093` - Offer a Spell Book when entering a Mage Guild town

> **Category:** Town UI

When a hero without a `Spell Book` first visits a town with an accessible `Mage Guild`, offer to buy a book at the normal [Mage Guild price](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Mage_guild). Identify the hero and cost in the prompt.

Provide **Buy**, **Remind me next time**, and **Never ask for this hero** choices. Remember the latter choice for that hero in saved games; the player can still buy a book manually. **Remind me next time** suppresses the prompt for the rest of the current visit and offers it again on a later visit to a qualifying town.

If the hero lacks sufficient gold, disable **Buy** and explain the cost. Respect scenario restrictions that prevent a hero from acquiring a spell book. A successful purchase uses the normal spell-learning flow and does not grant spells that the hero could not ordinarily learn.
