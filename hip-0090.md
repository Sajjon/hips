# `hip-0090` - Bookmark adventure-map locations

> **Category:** Adventure Map UI

Add a **Bookmark location** action to the right-click information for an **Adventure Map** tile. Let the player name and remove bookmarks for places such as a difficult battle, `Hill Fort`, `Magic Spring`, or waypoint, and retain the list in saved games.

Provide a **Bookmarks** list showing each location's name, map level, and the selected hero's estimated movement cost to reach it. Use the normal [movement and route calculation](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Movement), and distinguish unreachable or insufficiently known routes from valid estimates. Refresh costs when the hero or relevant route conditions change.

Selecting a bookmark centers the map on its tile and switches levels when necessary, without moving a hero. Retain normal exploration limits; the bookmark must not disclose changes behind fog of war.
