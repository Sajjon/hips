# `hip-0062` - Inspect connected map objects with right-click

> **Category:** Adventure Map UI

Extend the **Adventure Map** right-click information for `Monoliths`, `Subterranean Gates`, `Border Guards`, and `Keymaster's Tents` with a list of their known connected locations. Let the player center the map on a listed location, switching map levels when needed, while retaining the selected hero and route.

For a `Monolith`, show its valid exits, respecting one-way connections. For a `Subterranean Gate`, show its paired gate. For a `Border Guard`, show known matching tents; for a tent, show known matching guards. Follow the game's [connection rules](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Map_Editor_-_Objects#Subterranean_Gate,_Monolith_and_Whirlpool) and [tent colors](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Keymaster%27s_Tent).

List only locations and connections already disclosed to the player through normal exploration or an existing information effect. Mark an unknown destination as unknown rather than revealing it. Inspecting or centering a location must not move a hero or activate an object.
