# `hip-0099` - Open town construction with a hotkey

> **Category:** Town UI

Add a dedicated keyboard shortcut in the **Town Screen** to open that town's **Hall Screen**, where the player chooses buildings to construct. Show the shortcut in the corresponding control's tooltip.

Choose a key combination that is unassigned in the target build's town context. In particular, preserve `B` for the [Marketplace](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Marketplace), along with other existing town shortcuts.

Opening the screen must behave like clicking the town's hall, retaining normal building prerequisites, resource requirements, and daily construction limits. The shortcut opens the choices without constructing a building.
