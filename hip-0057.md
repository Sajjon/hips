# `hip-0057` - Skip stationary heroes during enemy turns

> **Category:** Adventure Map UI

During the enemy-turn animation, omit the visual turn segment for every enemy hero configured as stationary with a `Patrol` radius of `0`. Such a hero cannot leave its placement tile, so the game must not pan or center the **Adventure Map** on that hero, play an idle movement delay, or otherwise interrupt the animation sequence merely to show that the hero remains in place.

Do not hide or remove the hero from the **Adventure Map**. If the camera is already showing the hero for another reason, its sprite remains visible; this proposal only prevents a dedicated camera jump or no-op animation segment during enemy turns.

Apply the same omission to **Replay Opponent Turn** so the live enemy turn and its replay remain consistent. Continue to show any visible action that actually changes game state, even if it involves a stationary hero.

Use the hero's configured `Patrol` radius rather than observed movement for this decision. A mobile enemy hero that happens to stay on the same tile during one turn must not be classified as stationary or suppressed on later turns.
