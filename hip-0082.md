# `hip-0082` - Preview the second hex of breath attacks

> **Category:** Combat UI

While aiming a `Breath Attack`, highlight its secondary affected hex and any creature stack occupying it. Update the highlight whenever the intended attack direction, contact hex, or approach changes, including the direction-dependent cases for two-hex attackers.

Highlight friendly stacks as clearly as enemy stacks, since [breath attacks](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Breath_attack) can damage either side on the second hex. Derive the preview from the exact planned attack geometry rather than assuming a fixed hex behind the target's center. A two-hex creature occupying both affected hexes must not be presented as taking damage twice.

Show the secondary hex even when it is empty. The preview must not alter the attack or apply damage until the player confirms it.
