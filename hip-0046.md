# `hip-0046` - Show terrain movement and combat effects

> **Category:** Adventure Map UI

While a hero is selected on the **Adventure Map**, extend the existing `Alt`-hover movement preview to show a **Terrain effects** panel for the hovered tile. Show the terrain's name, its base off-road movement cost, and the effective movement cost for the selected hero after applying the route, roads, `Pathfinding`, army composition, and other active movement effects. When a penalty is reduced or removed, name the reason rather than showing only the final number.

Also show the combat effects that would apply if a battle were fought on that tile. Identify every stack in the selected hero's army that is native to the terrain and show its `+1 Attack`, `+1 Defense`, and `+1 Speed` bonuses. For example, hovering over `Snow` must show these bonuses for native `Tower` creatures. Include any visible magical-terrain effects that would modify or suppress the native-terrain bonuses.

Keep road movement separate from the underlying terrain's combat effects: a road may replace the movement cost without changing which native-terrain bonuses apply in combat. Update the panel immediately when the selected hero, army, route, or hovered tile changes. If no hero is selected, show the terrain's base movement cost and native towns without claiming any hero-specific effects.
