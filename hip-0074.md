# `hip-0074` - Inspect the player's hero during combat

> **Category:** Combat UI

During combat, right-clicking the player's own hero must open a read-only view of that hero's equipped `artifacts`, primary and secondary skills, and `specialty`. Make this available while the player can normally inspect the battlefield, including the `Tactics phase`.

Show the hero's current combat-relevant values and the normal descriptions of their equipment and skills. Close the information view back to the same battle state without issuing a spell or creature command. Equipment and skills cannot be changed through this view.

Keep this shortcut limited to information about the player's own hero. Preserve the normal [combat controls](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Keybinds#Combat_Screen) and information restrictions for opposing heroes.
