# `hip-0101` - Use legal final diagonal steps in automatic routes

> **Category:** Adventure Map UI

When following a plotted route, let automatic movement take the route's next diagonal step if the game's existing last-step rule makes it legal with the hero's remaining `movement points`. The route display and movement executor must agree that the tile is reachable today.

Apply the same [final diagonal-step calculation](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Movement) as manual movement, including the active terrain, roads, and movement modifiers. Continue toward the chosen destination along the legal route; do not substitute an unrelated diagonal tile merely to exhaust the hero's points.

This reuses an existing movement allowance. It must not grant extra movement or relax normal passability, interaction, or combat restrictions.
