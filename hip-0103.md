# `hip-0103` - Show whether duplicate artifact effects stack

> **Category:** Hero UI — artifacts

Add a **Duplicate effects** line to every `artifact` description, stating whether its effects stack when the same hero equips multiple copies of that exact artifact. Show this information wherever the normal artifact description is available, including equipped slots, the backpack, hero exchanges, and purchase or reward previews, so the player can check before acquiring or equipping a duplicate.

Use **Stacks** when each equipped copy contributes its effect, **Does not stack** when additional equipped copies provide no additional effect, and **Cannot equip multiple copies** when equipment-slot restrictions prevent the same hero from equipping duplicates. For artifacts with multiple effects or special limits, explain the rule for each affected effect and state any cap or condition. Cover every artifact available in *Horn of the Abyss*, including `combination artifacts`.

For example, two equipped copies of `Endless Purse of Gold` produce `1,000 gold` per day in total, whereas two equipped copies of `Loins of Legion` provide the same creature-growth bonus as one copy. These rules are documented on the wiki's [artifact overview](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Artifact), [Endless Purse of Gold](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Endless_Purse_of_Gold), and [Loins of Legion](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Loins_of_Legion) pages.

Make clear that the line concerns identical artifacts equipped by one hero; carrying additional copies in the backpack does not count as equipping them. Preserve the existing stacking rules and describe the behavior of the installed game version.
