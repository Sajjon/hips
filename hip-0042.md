# `hip-0042` - Skip the `Hut of the Magi` reveal animation

> **Category:** Adventure Map UI

While the camera is showing the areas revealed by visiting a `Hut of the Magi`, let the player press `Esc`, `Enter`, or `Space` to end the remaining reveal animation immediately and return the camera and controls to the visiting hero. Skipping the animation must still reveal the areas around every associated `Eye of the Magi`; it skips only the presentation, not the gameplay effect. Consume the keypress so it does not also trigger an adventure-map command.
