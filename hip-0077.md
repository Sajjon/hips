# `hip-0077` - Show current hero specialty bonuses

> **Category:** Hero UI

In the **Hero Screen**, right-clicking the hero's **Specialty** icon must show the actual bonus at the hero's current level, alongside the usual specialty description.

For creature specialties, identify the affected creature types and show their current specialty-derived bonuses. For secondary-skill or spell specialties, show the current calculated modifier and explain any dependency on skill proficiency or target creature level. For fixed bonuses and upgrade specialties, describe their actual fixed effect rather than inventing level scaling.

Follow the active game's [hero specialty rules](https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Hero_specialty), including *Horn of the Abyss* differences. Refresh the values when the hero gains a level or another relevant input changes.
